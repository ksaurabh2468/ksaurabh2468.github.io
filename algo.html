Principles

How I work

Stage 1

Replay

Recorded market data, replayed deterministically. Every state tested on purpose — not just the ones that happened.

Stage 2

Paper

Live data, simulated orders. Production behaviour, no money at risk.

Stage 3

Live

Reduced size, kill switch armed, staged ramp — with a rollback plan agreed before cutover.

Evidence, not assurances. Every requirement traces to a test, and every test to a recorded, inspectable result — at every stage above.
